Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 01, 2026. Your privacy is our priority. Learn how we handle your data responsibly.

1. Data Collection

We collect information only when you voluntarily provide it, such as when you fill out our contact form or subscribe to our newsletter. This may include your name, email address, and any messages you send us.

The information we collect is used solely to:

We use essential cookies to ensure our platform works correctly and to remember your theme preferences (Dark/Light mode). We also use basic analytics to understand how our site is used and to improve our courses.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, to request corrections, or to ask us to delete your data at any time. To do so, please contact us via our contact page.
