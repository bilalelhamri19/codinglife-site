Productivity Tips

Practical ways to increase your daily output and work smarter, not harder.

The 80/20 Rule

The Pareto Principle states that 80% of your results come from 20% of your efforts. Identify those high-impact tasks and prioritize them above everything else.

Deep Work

Schedule blocks of 90 minutes for "Deep Work" where you turn off all notifications. This is where real progress is made, especially in complex tasks like coding.

Tip: Try the Pomodoro technique (25 min work, 5 min break) if you're struggling to stay focused.

Eliminate Distractions

Your phone is the biggest enemy of productivity. Keep it in another room or use focus modes to ensure you stay in the flow state.
