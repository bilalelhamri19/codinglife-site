Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting your career with Coding Life.

Do I need a computer science degree to start?

Absolutely not. While a degree can be helpful, the tech industry primarily values skills and projects. Many of the world's most successful developers are self-taught. Our platform is designed to provide you with the practical skills that companies actually look for during the hiring process.

How long does it take to become job-ready?

It depends on your dedication, but most students who commit 10-15 hours a week can reach a junior developer level in 6 to 9 months. Our curriculum is optimized to cut out the "noise" and focus on the high-impact skills that will get you hired faster.

Is there a community I can join?

Yes, we believe that community is the secret sauce to success. You can join our global Discord server where thousands of developers share tips, collaborate on projects, and provide support when you're stuck on a bug. Learning alone is hard; learning together is much more efficient.

What technologies should I start with?

For 90% of beginners, we recommend starting with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. These are the building blocks of the web and allow you to see visual results immediately. Once you have a solid grasp of these, you can branch out into specialized fields like mobile development or data science.
